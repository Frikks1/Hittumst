# Community guidelines

Hittumst is for consenting adults aged 18 or older. Treat every person with dignity, respect stated
boundaries, and stop contacting someone when they ask you to stop.

## Private albums

- Share only media you own or have the right to share. Everyone shown must be an adult and must have
  consented to both the recording and the sharing.
- Never upload or share content involving a person under 18, non-consensual intimate imagery,
  secretly recorded media, sexual exploitation, coercion, trafficking, extortion, or illegal acts.
- A private album reduces exposure; it does not make copying impossible. Do not save, copy,
  redistribute, or threaten to redistribute another member's private media.
- Album access may be declined or revoked at any time. Attempts to bypass expiry, view-once access,
  blocks, or screenshot protections are prohibited.
- Report suspected minors, non-consensual intimate imagery, threats, extortion, or other illegal
  material immediately. Do not download or redistribute it while reporting.

## Profiles and tags

Use official profile tags honestly. Tags do not grant consent and never override a boundary stated
in a profile or conversation. Harassment, hate, impersonation, spam, fraud, and discriminatory use
of sensitive profile information are prohibited.

## Hittingar

- Hosts must describe the event and location honestly, choose an appropriate general area, classify
  explicit-adult content, and provide arrival instructions that do not put participants at risk.
- Hittingar may not advertise paid or compensated sexual services, trafficking or exploitation,
  illegal activity, coercion, threats, discrimination, spam, or deceptive locations. There are no
  payments, tickets, or pricing in the feature.
- Joining or approval is not consent to sexual activity, photography, recording, substance use, or
  any change of venue. Consent must be specific, informed, freely given, and reversible.
- Use Private access and delayed Protected location for homes or sensitive gatherings. Open +
  Protected lets any eligible account join and later see the address. Removing or blocking someone
  stops future access but cannot erase an address they already saw, remembered, or captured.
- Do not publish another person's home or workplace without their permission. Do not copy, share,
  scrape, or use a meetup address outside the purpose for which the host disclosed it.
- Report suspected minors, coercion, exploitation, credible threats, dangerous or misleading
  locations, hate/discrimination, illegal activity, compensated sexual services, and material
  misrepresentation. In an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services first.
