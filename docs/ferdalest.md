# Ferðalest and daily discovery

Ferðalest is a permanent group for choosing Hittingar and going together. The nearby profile grid remains the landing screen. The new tab creates private, invite-only trains; invitations reuse accepted friendships and the existing consent flow. Owners/admins can edit the name, symbol, description and public visibility. Public trains expose a directory card and permit joining; chat, media and location records remain restricted to members. Existing group voice uses the configured LiveKit integration.

Members can recommend gatherings, add a note, mark their intention to go, open the event to RSVP, read reviews and discuss the plan in chat. Intending to go with a train does not itself RSVP to the gathering. Trains do not expire.

Camera capture and library uploads can be sent to multiple groups and people from friends/recent chats. A direct conversation can also launch capture with its recipient selected. Images, videos and animated GIFs pass through the existing quarantine/moderation worker. GIFs are normalized to MP4 to preserve animation. Videos are limited to 60 seconds and 50 MiB. Owner/admin uploads can also form the group's cover gallery. Uploaded media does not have a disappearing-message timer. Native camera and voice need a signed device build for verification.

Exact location is an explicit, foreground snapshot, visible for 15 or 60 minutes to selected current members. It is off by default, can be stopped early, and does not extend to new members. Leaving/removal revokes the member's outgoing share and removes them from incoming shares. Public visibility never makes locations public. No background tracking is introduced.

Discovery keeps an account-bound selection in Postgres. Free: 20 profiles; provisional paid limits: 60 for Flottari plebbi and 120 for Plebba Kóngur. A deliberate refresh replaces the selection with different candidates at most once per Icelandic calendar day. Ordinary refresh, pagination, filter changes and reopening the app reuse the same selection. Filters narrow that selection. Paid limits are checked against authoritative subscriptions. Upgrades expand the selection; downgrades restrict the visible IDs. Too few eligible new candidates results in fewer profiles, rather than recycling the current selection. Update both `DISCOVERY_PROFILE_LIMITS` and a follow-up database migration when changing these product limits.

## Train sponsorship pools

Train pools use the existing balanced, lot-backed commerce ledger. Contributions debit a member's wallet and credit the train's shared balance; they never mint funds. A public train can also accept a nonmember's sponsorship without giving that sponsor access to chat, locations, balances or allocation details. Externally sponsored trains display a badge. Contributions belong to the train and cannot be withdrawn by their donors or administrators. Archiving is rejected while the train has a balance or unsettled sponsorships, so it cannot strand shared funds or later refunds. This change does not block account-deletion requests; requesting deletion stops that administrator's automatic spending and flags the retained pool for review.

Owners and admins configure automatic sponsorship and a monthly budget. The commerce worker funds each planned gathering once when at least one current member both marks their intention to go with the train and holds a confirmed RSVP (or hosts the event). The server rechecks the plan, actual RSVP, visibility, membership, administrator authority and ledger revision under locks before committing. Marking an intention alone does not spend the pool. A 10% fee is added to the chosen sponsorship and counts toward the monthly cap. The mobile settings authorize a 25% host / 75% participant split; events with a different split are skipped. Insufficient balance, exhausted budgets, closed events and inactive groups do not spend. Existing sponsorship settlement and cancellation handling apply, with cancellation principal and fee returned to the train pool. Group contributions do not give the configuring administrator personal sponsor priority or appear as personally reversible wallet contributions.

All money remains **sandbox-only**, matching the repository's existing commerce gate. These are operational wallet and event-pool ledger transfers in that sandbox, not production payments. Production custody, deposits and payouts still require the app's payment-provider launch work. No paid placement or advertising auction is added.

## Applying and verifying

Apply all three Ferðalest migrations together with this client/admin build. Keep the media and commerce workers running; uploads are not delivered before inspection, and the commerce worker processes automatic train sponsorship. Legacy isolated test-unit deposits are disabled and their balances are never converted into wallet funds. The RPCs use actor-bound private implementations; private tables have RLS and no client grants. No production database was changed while preparing this branch.

Run `npm run check` and the CI database jobs for clean installation and baseline upgrade. Database tests cover train privacy, consent revocation on removal/blocking, moderated media, public access, profile limits, daily refresh, finance authorization and stale RSVP rejection. Finance tests cover wallet conservation, fees, budgets, idempotence and refunds. Camera tests verify account isolation across asynchronous uploads. Native camera capture, permission prompts, location sharing and LiveKit voice still need verification on signed device builds before release.
